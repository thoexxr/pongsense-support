一句话版本：PongSense 不收集你的任何数据。视频只在你主动选择后于设备本机分析，不上传、不联网、不需要账号。

中文

PongSense（“本 App”）由个人开发者许星然（Xingran Xu）提供。本政策说明本 App 如何处理与你有关的信息。

1. 我们不收集任何数据

本 App 没有账号体系，不需要注册或登录，不收集姓名、邮箱、手机号、设备标识符、广告标识符、位置、通讯录等任何个人信息，也不接入任何第三方统计、广告或崩溃分析 SDK。

2. 视频

只有你在 App 内主动从相册选择某段视频后，本 App 才会读取该视频，并在你的设备上逐帧分析。视频不会上传到任何服务器；分析过程中产生的临时副本会在分析结束后删除。App 使用系统照片选择器，只访问你选中的那一段视频，不会浏览或读取你的整个相册。

3. 分析结果

球命中率、人物检出率、骨架关键点、动作技术统计等结果全部在设备本地计算，仅保存在设备上或显示在界面上；只有你主动点击“保存到相册”时，才会写入你的相册。App 不会把结果发送给任何第三方。

4. 网络

本 App 的核心分析功能完全离线运行，不发起网络请求，不上传视频、图像或分析结果。

5. 权限

本 App 仅在你主动选择视频时请求访问该视频（系统照片选择器），不请求位置、通讯录、麦克风、相机等权限。

6. 设备端模型与开源组件

本 App 内置三个 Core ML 模型文件并在设备本地推理：乒乓球检测模型 yolox.mlpackage（YOLOX-tiny，Apache License 2.0）、人体骨架模型 RTMO_Dense.mlpackage 与 RTMO_DCC.mlpackage（OpenMMLab mmpose 的 RTMO，Apache License 2.0）。这些模型只做本地计算，不联网、不回传任何数据，也不含任何用户数据。完整的组件清单、许可与来源见 模型与开源许可。

7. 儿童

本 App 不面向儿童收集任何信息（本 App 不收集任何人的信息），年龄分级为 4+。

8. 政策变更

若本 App 将来新增需要联网的功能（例如账号同步），本政策会在功能上线前更新，并在本页面标注新的更新日期。

9. 联系方式

如对本政策有疑问，请发送邮件至 thoexxr@hotmail.com，或通过 App Store 产品页的开发者联系方式与我们联系。

English

PongSense (the “App”) is provided by Xingran Xu, an individual developer. This policy explains how the App handles information about you.

1. We collect no data

There is no account system: no sign-up, no login. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, location, contacts, or any other personal information. The App contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs.

2. Videos

The App reads a video only after you explicitly pick one with the system photo picker, and processes it frame by frame on your device. Videos are never uploaded to any server; the temporary copy used for analysis is deleted when analysis ends. The App never scans or reads your entire photo library.

3. Results

Ball hit rate, person detection rate, skeleton key points and stroke statistics are computed locally and stay on your device. They are written to your photo library only when you tap “Save to Photos”.

4. Network

Core analysis runs fully offline. The App makes no network requests and never uploads videos, images, or results.

5. Permissions

The App accesses only the single video you explicitly select, through the system photo picker. It does not request location, contacts, microphone, or camera access.

6. On-device models and open-source components

Three Core ML models are bundled and run locally: the ball detector yolox.mlpackage (YOLOX-tiny, Apache License 2.0) and the human pose models RTMO_Dense.mlpackage / RTMO_DCC.mlpackage (OpenMMLab mmpose RTMO, Apache License 2.0). These models perform local inference only — no network access, no data returned to us, and no user data is contained in them. See Models & Open-Source Licenses for the full list, licenses and sources.

7. Children

The App collects no information from anyone, including children. App Store age rating: 4+.

8. Changes

If a future version adds network features (for example account sync), this policy will be updated before that feature ships and the date at the top will change.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy: thoexxr@hotmail.com, or the developer contact listed on the App Store product page.
